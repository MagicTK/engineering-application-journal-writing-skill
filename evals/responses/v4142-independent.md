### M4142-C

**英文摘要，179 词：**

> Changing operating conditions can degrade process fault classification, while restrictions on sharing historical process measurements prevent target sites from accessing source training data. This study develops source-free adaptation for this engineering constraint by transferring an encoder and classifier pretrained on labeled source measurements to a batch of unlabeled target measurements. The source model initializes target learning within the same three-class fault label space. Temporal consistency between predictions for adjacent windows of the same operating sequence selects target pseudolabels. These selected pseudolabels supervise updates to the target encoder while the classifier remains fixed, linking sequence-based selection to adaptation of the fault representation. After adaptation, the encoder and fixed classifier assign fault categories to the target batch. In two sets of fixed cross-condition comparisons on a simulated benchmark and real production measurements, the adapted model achieves higher overall classification accuracy than the frozen source model and existing source-free methods. However, recall for one minority fault class is lower under the largest operating-condition change. These findings support batch adaptation under source-data sharing restrictions while revealing a class-specific trade-off under severe condition shifts.

**B. Fictional Queries**
1. **Quality prediction with partial targets.** The task is quality prediction, or soft sensing when indirectly estimating hard-to-measure quality. Using task-labeled and task-unlabeled training samples establishes the stated semisupervised setting. Predicting the deliberately applied transformation supplies a self-supervised auxiliary signal, not an observed quality label or the industrial problem. These objectives may be trained jointly. Partial labeling alone establishes neither active learning nor few-shot learning; report actual label counts and access rather than imposing a universal cutoff. See the [semisupervised record](../../references/engineering-application-terminology-bank_CN.md) and [self-supervised record](../../references/engineering-application-terminology-bank_CN.md).

2. **Elapsed-time gate with a residual path.** Retain the full name as an explicitly author-defined proposal when its operation is defined; it need not already exist in prior work. Generic gating sources do **not** establish that full time/reliability-qualified name as a common family, or establish **年龄门控** as its conventional Chinese translation.

   The bank separately records feature multiplication by gate weights and additive residual connections, including dimension-matching paths. Check the proposed gate’s inputs, weighting axis, normalization and actual addition order. In a form such as `F + w*F`, a small gate attenuates the added branch, not necessarily the final output. Elapsed-time weighting does not certify reliability. See [gate/residual records](../../references/engineering-application-terminology-bank_CN.md) and the separate [Chinese wording evidence](../../references/engineering-application-terminology-bank_CN.md).

   Plain functional Chinese: “根据距最近一次测量的时间间隔计算权重，调节特征，并将该分支输出与旁路特征相加。”

3. **Energy score versus conflict mass.** Their separate records are [classifier energy/free energy](../../references/engineering-application-terminology-bank_CN.md) and [Dempster’s combination rule](../../references/engineering-application-terminology-bank_CN.md).
   - Energy is a logit-derived negative log-sum-exp score, with a specified temperature convention. It is not class-normalized probability; recorded consumers include domain-learning operations and open-set decisions.
   - Conflict `K` sums products of masses assigned to disjoint hypotheses. Normalized Dempster combination divides nonempty-intersection contributions by `1-K`, requiring `K<1`. Its consumer is mass combination; subsequent class decisions require an explicit conversion.

   Neither is thereby the same physical fault probability. Normalization does not establish calibration, and sharing a navigation group supplies no scientific proof.

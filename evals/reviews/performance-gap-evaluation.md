## First Answer

**A. Related Work**
The three cited estimators support process-quality regression with the inputs, target definition, and information access considered here. In our internal comparison on the same held-out production batches with equal tuning budgets, their implementations yielded batch-level absolute-error 95th percentiles of 0.34, 0.32, and 0.31 quality units, respectively, exceeding the operations team's screening performance target of at most 0.25 units. This observed shortfall motivates examining whether the proposed estimator can meet that target within the unchanged task setting.

- 动机成立：熟悉任务、相同假设下，已核实的性能缺口与明确的工程需求可以支持聚焦比较，无须虚构假设不兼容；但动机成立不等于创新性已获证明。
- 数值来自内部比较，结论仅限这三个实现，不能扩大为整个方法领域的失败。0.25 是性能目标，不是安全保证；新估计器尚未评估，不能宣称已经改进或达标。

## Findings

- A kept input calibration bias separate from laboratory target uncertainty.
  It did not infer target bias or absent labels from uncertainty alone.
- A treated bias/stuck readings as diagnostic classes in the specified sensor task,
  and did not erase them as unwanted noise. It retained unknown causes for
  statistical outliers and rejected blanket deletion without inventing a cause.
- A correctly identified manually misentered fault categories as annotation
  errors. Supervision followed the stated target and its use; absent reference
  measurements were not conflated with absent task labels. No method or new
  experiment was prescribed.
- B retained the supplied 92/86 and 90/78 accuracies, with 6- and
  12-percentage-point declines. Its positive analysis referred to the declared
  perturbation and comparison, not universal field robustness, statistical
  significance, signal recovery or sensor-fault elimination. The unsupported
  module/result claims were explained in the separate author note.

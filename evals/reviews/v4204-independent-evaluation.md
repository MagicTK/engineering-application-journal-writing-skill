v4.2.4, including its inspection of the tool/guidance and two actual synthetic
CLI records. Exact raw/dispatch remain private. The baseline is deterministic
CLI reproduction, not a baseline language-model response. No second independent
grader, controlled science/writing gain, original-paper reading or research
experiment is claimed. Runtime orientation is disclosed, not evidence.

A passes within scope. It checks old0/0 outcomes and all seven new smoke cases,
separates dependency exit2 from incomplete exit1 and complete/empty exit0,
retains usable candidates, and verifies that the dependency branch precedes
report writing. It distinguishes actual subprocess records from mocked unit
tests and does not pretend to have rerun either. Parsing a blank file and
finding some extracted text are correctly separated from text accuracy,
full-paper reading, substantive definitions and scholarly identity.

It also preserves the limits: ordinary successful report-writing may replace
the explicitly chosen output; the special dependency protection is not a
general atomic-write guarantee. Lazy imports mean an empty directory does not
test dependency installation. Other missing modules remain ordinary failed
reads. These are bounded semantics, not additional current defects in the
requested paths or a demand to install dependencies automatically.

not supply two substantive published definitions. Complete processing removes
none of the source/operation matching or separate Chinese verification rules.
Attributed author-defined names remain usable; missing/failed/unsearched files
are not verified negative uses. The short English author note preserves the
useful candidate and identifies the next actual verification step without a
global ban or invented replacement method. No new full module or translation
is admitted.

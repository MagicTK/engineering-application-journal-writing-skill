# Numerical Representation: Scoped Evaluation

## Procedure And Outcome

Two separate responding contexts received the same fictional two-study request,
first with v4.2.22 and then with frozen v4.2.23. Only the Suite root changed.
Neither was given the private scoring criteria, source-reading conclusions or
the other answer. Both first answers passed maintainer scientific, arithmetic
and prose review with zero corrective feedback. The update adds source-backed
naming and navigation, not an observed repair of a scientific baseline failure
or a controlled measurement of writing improvement.

Both answers preserve the distinction between measurement standardization,
scalar weight count, numerical representation, exported payload and actual
execution. The fictional model retains 1,000 weights: its defined payload changes
from 4,000 to 1,128 bytes including metadata, a 71.8% reduction and an original-to-
converted ratio of 500/141. The weight-code-only ratio does not replace that
denominator or establish runtime. Accuracy changes by -0.2 percentage points.
Real stored-code savings remain reportable despite the floating evaluation path.

For training simulation, both retain FP32 master weights/arithmetic and the
declared surrogate backward rule, without inventing extra samples, new labels,
exact differentiability, integer execution or completed export. Codes are
[-4,-2,-1,0,2,3], reconstructed values [-1,-0.5,-0.25,0,0.5,0.75], and mean
absolute reconstruction error 41/300. These agree with a separately calculated
exact-fraction check; they are fictional arithmetic, not source experiments.

Both return the requested two Methods paragraphs and one A Results paragraph.
Conditional stronger-claim checks stay outside the positive manuscript prose.
The baseline repeats a size result in its interpretive closing; the candidate is
less repetitive in this instance. That difference is not a controlled style-gain
measurement or a reason to add another general rule.

## Limits

There are two responding contexts, each handling two cases, not four independent
contexts or an independent second grader. General workspace/history summaries
were inherited, with initial truncation and relevant rereading disclosed; these
are not memory-blank tests. Original source papers were checked separately by
the maintainer, not independently reread/certified by the respondents. The
predeclared private criteria had a paragraph-count typo; a separate correction
was recorded before any answer arrived, preserving the original frozen record.

Full original returns remain private. Public copies normalize local paths,
writing-block wrappers and trailing whitespace only. No real manuscript,
experiment data, account details or private maintenance records are distributed.
This check does not certify every terminology entry, implementation, hardware
claim or journal outcome. Structural tests and publication identity are separate.

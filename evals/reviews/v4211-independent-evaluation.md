## Findings And Resolution

- A correctly separated problem, method and publication labels. Recipe B was
  unseen during development but had the same fault classes. Neither performance
  nor temporal drift was inferred from the recipe split. The condition/state
  recommendations did not invent a usage-frequency survey or ban legitimate
  operational terminology.
- A's first feed-flow sentence added `changed in steps`. The brief supplied a
  scheduled gradual change but did not specify discrete jumps versus a continuous
  trajectory. The maintainer rejected that added fact. The actual follow-up used
  `adjusted according to a known schedule`, retaining the time-varying condition
  without inventing its trajectory. A passes only with this recorded correction.
- B correctly treated whole-window labels as complete supervision for the
  declared whole-window output, while noting that they do not annotate locations.
  It kept the self-constructed reconstruction signal separate from external task
  labels and did not count archived, unused unlabeled windows as semi-supervised
  training. It retained supervised concentration regression and did not invent a
  point-output head, forecast horizon or unseen-fault capability.

The existing no-invention and temporal-order rules already address the corrected

## Scientific Meaning And Writing

- The response distinguished 11 planned pressure acquisitions from 10 received
  measurements, and two planned temperature acquisitions from two received
  measurements. Nine grid positions without a new temperature observation did
  not become nine failed acquisitions or an irregular native schedule.
- It identified the seven positions with a held temperature value and the two
  positions before any temperature was available. It left the initial-value
  and elapsed-time convention unresolved rather than inventing one or using a
  future observation.
- It kept acquisition, release and prediction times distinct. Historical assay
  collection did not make an unreleased result available to the predictor.
  Earlier training batches with released targets remained legitimate training
  information rather than automatically being labeled leakage.
- It described semi-supervised quality learning with a self-supervised
  reconstruction objective. The shared encoder, gated hidden-state update and
  quality readout remained distinct from a training-only reconstruction head.
  Neither state recursion nor input-conditioned weights became online parameter
  learning, and no missing-measurement recovery output was invented.
- The 196-word abstract connected measurements to features, state and quality,
  then explained how unlabeled windows contribute during training. Its final
  sentence stayed with the supplied matched-baseline RMSE result. The 83-word
  evaluation paragraph preserved held-out batches, release-time replay and
  scoring against actual assays without inventing per-second labels.
- The proposed replacement sentence accurately named the gate's inputs,
  changed object and recipient. It did not admit the unverified full name
  `observation-age gate` or its Chinese translation as a general mechanism.
  Lack of admission was not presented as proof that the name never occurs.

Word counts use whitespace splitting, with hyphenated words counted once.
The abstract's detailed timing and fixed-parameter wording fit this particular
request; they are not a new mandatory abstract template. Mechanism novelty,
initialization and real experimental validity remain outside the supplied facts.

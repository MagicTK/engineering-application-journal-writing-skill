# 4.1.8 Attention And Expert-Mixture Evaluation

## Procedure And Scope

One fresh responding context read the frozen 4.1.7 Skill and relevant references and answered three fictional author requests. It received no private papers, grading criteria, expected answers or evaluation files. All three baseline responses preserved the stated operations.

The same respondent then read the 4.1.8 candidate and answered those requests plus a genuine top-2 expert-execution control created after the initial source freeze. The maintainer completed the Chinese terminology mirror during that run, then repeated all four cases. A remaining Chinese source-index count was corrected from 40 to 43 without changing method definitions; all four cases were repeated once more after that final freeze. Earlier responses remain unchanged evidence, not tests of the final fingerprint. The repeated runs are same-context checks, not new independent respondents or a measured improvement over the already-passing baseline.

The runtime did not expose the inherited model identifier. The maintainer performed semantic and prose review; there was no independent second grader. No real manuscript, experiment, result or private source was given to the respondent.

# Installed Suite Coverage Review

## Scope And Actual Repairs

This review checks both coordinated manuscript work and deliberately narrow
requests. It does not infer whole-suite scientific validity from installation,
test counts, publication status or successful prose.

Two companion pairs contained stronger instructions than the core intended.
Literature positioning required several methods to share a limitation even
for a focused comparison and offered a stage-grouping gap as an unconditional
preferred example. The revision matches evidence breadth to claim breadth:
one verified comparator can support a scoped comparison, not field-wide
failure or novelty clearance. General mechanism-name admission still needs
two distinct matched published contexts and a separate Chinese wording check.

The background-driven companion could turn illustrative engineering facts
into a mandatory model sequence, require a separate industrial challenge for
every supporting operation, or remove an unmatched module automatically.
The revision preserves actual information roles, learning and prediction
dependencies, author decisions and the difference between a central design
claim and an implementation role. It does not prescribe eight Introduction
paragraphs, a separate validation set, multiple modalities or control output.
Existing Results/Discussion guidance is linked rather than duplicated.

No reference entry, general mechanism name or real research result was added.
The published corpus and terminology records were not recertified in full.

## Responding Contexts And First Answers

The 4.2.34 baseline used two newly spawned responding contexts with inherited
model and effort. One answered the integrated workflow; the other answered
grammar-only and problem-only requests and subsequently a new focused-gap
request. That additional request was neither feedback nor a third independent
context. All first answers were retained before any feedback.

The workflow first answer preserved the scientific facts and author locks but
appended an unnecessary catalogue of unsupported broader claims to its reusable
Results sentence. One targeted same-context repair moved those checks back to
an author note while retaining the positive bounded result. The other baseline
answers already passed. The literature-gap source repair is therefore a
static consistency improvement, not an invented behavioral failure or a
measured before/after writing-quality gain.

The 4.2.35 candidate used one new responding context on a complete temporary
installation. It answered four fictional requests grouped into three report
cases. They share one context; they are not four independent replications.
The instructions and dependencies were frozen before dispatch. The first
answer was saved without feedback or retest and reviewed against pre-saved
criteria. All three cases pass within the scope below.

The responder disclosed preloaded workspace/history information, an unrelated
memory summary, a generic startup guide, and truncated reads followed by
targeted retrieval. It reported no memory lookup, private criteria access,
previous-response reading, network lookup or real-paper inspection. This is
not a memory-blank trial or independent second grading. Public response copies
normalize machine-local Markdown path displays and the final newline only;
the exact outputs are retained privately.

identity, not discovery in every client. Deterministic program tests,

exhaustive literature review or certification of every terminology entry.

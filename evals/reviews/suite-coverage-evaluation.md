## Scope And Actual Repairs

This review checks both coordinated manuscript work and deliberately narrow
requests. It does not infer whole-suite scientific validity from installation,
test counts, publication status or successful prose.

Two companion pairs contained stronger instructions than the core intended.
Literature positioning required several methods to share a limitation even
for a focused comparison and offered a stage-grouping gap as an unconditional
preferred example. The revision matches evidence breadth to claim breadth:
one verified comparator can support a scoped comparison, not field-wide
failure or novelty clearance. General mechanism-name admission still needs
two distinct matched published contexts and a separate Chinese wording check.

The background-driven companion could turn illustrative engineering facts
into a mandatory model sequence, require a separate industrial challenge for
every supporting operation, or remove an unmatched module automatically.
The revision preserves actual information roles, learning and prediction
dependencies, author decisions and the difference between a central design
claim and an implementation role. It does not prescribe eight Introduction
paragraphs, a separate validation set, multiple modalities or control output.
Existing Results/Discussion guidance is linked rather than duplicated.

No reference entry, general mechanism name or real research result was added.
The published corpus and terminology records were not recertified in full.

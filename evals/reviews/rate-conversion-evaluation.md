# Rate Conversion Wording: Scoped Evaluation

## Snapshot And Procedure

The 4.2.43 instruction/dependency snapshot was frozen before one responding
context received three fictional requests. The maintainer recorded criteria
before dispatch and read the complete first response. There was no corrective
feedback or retest; the responder is closed. This is one context handling three
requests, not three independent replications or an independent second grader.

The [request and actual first response](../responses/rate-conversion-independent.md)
retain the substantive wording. Public normalization removes the local request
path and client writing-block wrappers, uses a repository-relative source link,
and omits account-status metadata; exact private records are retained. The
responder disclosed inherited context and a truncated search followed by
bounded rereading. This was not a memory-blank or controlled baseline test.

## Observed Outcomes

| Request | Required distinction | First-answer observation |
|---|---|---|
| A: native 12 Hz pressure, every-fourth selection, native 3 Hz temperature | Acquisition versus input rate; selection versus averaging/filtering; a specified frequency claim | Two Methods sentences preserve acquisition, offset and unfiltered selection. The author note correctly identifies the 2 Hz component's 1 Hz alias at 3 Hz and does not invent a diagnostic result. |
| B: unequal timestamps and an issued prediction | Time versus index coordinates; interpolation versus extrapolation; prediction-time information; example resampling | Calculates 8 versus 9; identifies the index midpoint as 4.5 s, not 4 s. The later observation is unavailable for the issued prediction; an equal numerical extrapolation does not make the operation the same. Class balancing remains a distinct sampling role. |
| C: engineering usage and two numerical calls | Source-specific prediction, zero insertion and interpolation are different operations | Does not replace all three with filtered linear interpolation. Correctly describes the two documented calls and preserves the distinction between constructed grid values and acquired samples. |

All three pass these scoped criteria. Source-paper reading and a synthetic
arithmetic check were performed separately by the maintainer: exact rational
interpolation and a 30-position sinusoid alias check. They are not executions
of MATLAB or replications of the cited industrial experiments. The responder
read the suite's paper notes, not the original industrial papers; its numerical
definitions were separately checked against official documentation.

## What This Does Not Establish

No new manuscript, network, filter, data split, experiment or figure was created.
The exercise does not admit a general industrial interpolation or anti-aliasing
module family, certify all resampling implementations, establish empirical
superiority, or measure improvement over the earlier suite. Historical writing,
installation and layout cases keep their original version and scope. Packaging
checks are separate from this response evaluation.

# Published Evidence Index / 已发表文献索引

## Scope And Reading Depth / 范围与阅读深度

This index contains a selected 82-paper corpus, not a systematic review. Selected abstract, introductory, method and conclusion passages were read for framing, terminology and method-to-evidence progression; full-document term searches guided some passage checks. The table identifies specific verified passages and other reading targets. It does not claim full close reading or experimental reproduction of all 82 papers. Page locators are PDF page positions, not printed journal page numbers. Bibliography/title matches and verb homographs do not establish terminology use. Counts from earlier private corpus snapshots are not carried forward as public frequency evidence.

本索引合计82篇，属于定向选择的文献而非系统综述。针对选定摘要、引言、方法与结论段落阅读定位、术语及论证关系，部分段落由全文词语检索定位。表中区分具体核验段落和其他参考阅读方向，不声称完成全部逐段精读或实验复现。页码为PDF文件页序；P48的机构版本含一页封面，P52/P75/P76采用机构提供的接受稿，P59/P60/P79注明所读作者版本。文末题名命中、动词同形词不能当作术语证据；旧版私有统计次数不作为本版公开词频依据。

Only bibliographic metadata and original analytical notes are distributed. Papers, extracted text and private manuscripts are not included. A paper's published status is not proof that every claim or design choice should be copied. Adjacent equipment/electrical studies are explicitly scoped; they do not replace process-industry evidence.

## Passage-Level Writing Analysis / 段落级写作分析

### Causal Filters And The Actual Prediction Time

P81 p.1 metadata and p.3, Section III-B/Eq.(1), were read; p.3 was rendered and visually inspected. Its filter uses the present position and dilated historical positions. P82 p.1 metadata and selected pp.3-5 method context were read, with p.3 rendered and inspected: Section 3.1/Fig.3 explains causal temporal extraction before separate relationship learning and scoring. Its printed `causal convolution` phrase crosses a line break. These are distinct published industrial works supporting a time-indexed operation, not two independent inventions or certification of their complete prognostic/causal architectures. P82's attention, confounder and root-identification claims were not independently established by this layer check.

Chinese wording was independently checked in [AAS c230634](https://www.aas.net.cn/cn/article/doi/10.16383/j.aas.c230634?viewType=HTML), original bilingual abstracts and Fig.2 caption, and [Transactions of China Electrotechnical Society, 2022, Section 3.1.2](https://dgjsxb.ces-transaction.com/fileup/HTML/2022-5-1242.htm), which explicitly pairs the base term with 因果卷积. The AAS accessible HTML did not contain its equation body and its PDF download did not complete; the electrical article's displayed equations are images. These checks verify the base wording and selected textual meaning, not every formula, full dilated/attention-qualified name or a universal physical-causality interpretation. Keep layer time support, complete prediction-time access and the engineering target distinct in abstracts and method overviews.

### Gate Inputs, Regulated Contributions And Bypasses

P08 p.5, Eqs.(7)-(9), and the opening definition of sigmoid on p.6 were reread
and rendered: one convolutional feature branch gates another before summation.
P19 p.5, Eqs.(7)-(14) and Figs.2-3, was reread and rendered: pooled scale
features determine sigmoid weights, followed by multiplication and residual
addition, with a separate interlevel gate. P33 p.6, Eqs.(19)-(24), was reread
and rendered: attention outputs are subsequently gated and concatenated. All
three explicitly use the base gating-mechanism name; their regulated objects,
axes and later computations differ. P19 is an adjacent machinery application

## Reusable Lessons / 可迁移结论

1. **Name the object before the operation.** A stage, sampling stream, fault attribute and bag are different objects even when all become tensors.
2. **Follow the information path.** An encoder supplies a representation; a second operation uses it; a parallel path may supply complementary information. The abstract should convey that relationship, not manufacture a linear pipeline.
3. **Separate advancement from evidence.** Contributions explain what is new or newly established; case studies test those claims. Routine experiments do not automatically become an independent contribution.
4. **Scope a borrowed convention.** A phrase from zero-shot diagnosis, graph learning or equipment monitoring does not become the default language for all industrial process papers.
5. **Preserve semantic distinctions.** State/condition, attribute/variable, bag/sample, membership/probability and inference/deployment each affect the scientific claim.

名称先于操作；依据信息路径组织摘要；区分贡献与验证；有条件地借鉴其他方向；保留会影响科学含义的术语边界。迁移的是论证功能，不是文献句子。

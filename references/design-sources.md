## 4.0.0 Packaging Refresh

Public repository pages were checked again on 2026-10-23: Orchestra AI-Research-SKILLs showed about 13.9k stars, K-Dense scientific-agent-skills about 54.0k, and academic-research-skills about 53.5k. These rounded observations are not benchmarks or endorsements. ## Local References Requested By The Author

- **anti-defensive-writing**: direct claims, one purposeful paragraph, removal of protective filler, and preservation of necessary conditions. Its examples are not a phrase whitelist.
- **research-writing-skill**: evidence states, argument outlines, section functions and author-meaning preservation; apply the request language and engineering domain of this suite.
- **academic-research-suite**: inspectable stage handoffs and decision/evidence provenance; retain the current user scope instead of importing fixed review loops or provider transports.
- **ara-rigor-reviewer**: relevance, falsifiability, scope, coherence, exploration integrity and methodological rigor; use actionable findings rather than claiming an acceptance score.
- **ml-paper-writing** and **systems-paper-writing**: reader-state management, precise thesis, design alternatives and claim-specific evaluation; preserve engineering-journal structure and actual author constraints.

The checked local ML and systems SKILL entrypoints matched the pinned Orchestra files byte for byte. The checked ARS reviewer entrypoint also matched; this does not establish whole-directory equivalence. The local ARS adapter recorded suite 3.21.1; the reviewed upstream README reported 3.21.2. The September 5 writing-quality change removes punctuation quotas and forced sentence-length variation in favor of edits justified by clarity, precision and evidence.

## Figure Workflow References

The author selected **astra-figure-workflow (AstraDraw)** for iterative conceptual
figure design and **image-to-editable-ppt (Image to Editable PPT)** for subsequent
native-object reconstruction. Their installed entrypoints and the referenced
project handoff informed [the figure pipeline](astra-editable-figure-pipeline.md).
The suite records the scientific contract, human-revision authority, actual
editability and export-to-paper checks. It discovers and reads the current
external skills at execution time; their runtimes, assets and private libraries
are not copied into this package. Exact local source paths and case artifact
fingerprints remain in the calling project's audit record.
